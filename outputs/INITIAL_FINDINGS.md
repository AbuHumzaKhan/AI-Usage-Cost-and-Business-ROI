# Initial Findings

These findings come from a synthetic dataset built to demonstrate the analysis workflow. They are not evidence of actual AI spend or productivity at a real organization.

- The sample contains 1,800 work tasks and 3,513 AI requests across six teams.
- Modeled AI request and allocated tool cost totals approximately ₹4,907.92 under the project's illustrative pricing assumptions.
- Customer Support has the lowest modeled cost per accepted AI-assisted output in this sample (about ₹2.72); Operations is about ₹3.05, Marketing ₹3.88, Finance ₹4.07, Development ₹4.59, and Research ₹5.43.
- These team figures are not directly comparable as unit economics because each team produces a different kind of output.
- The next validation step is to replace illustrative prices with provider billing data and confirm the accepted-output definition with each team's business owner.

## Interpretation limits
The sample is synthetic and observational. The estimated labor-capacity value is not equivalent to cash savings, and no causal productivity claim should be made from this dataset.
